Acknowledgments

Gabbai makes use of the HebCal Core integration. Hebrew dates, parshiyot, and occassions (yotnifs, special shabbatot) are provided according to the Hebrew calendar provided by Hebcal.

Verse finding for readings for the traditional reading schedule is powered by data compilied from HebCal. Verse finding for the triennial reading schedule is powered by data compiled from "A Complete Triennial System for Reading the Torah" by Rabbi Richard Eisenberg and published by the Rabbinical Assembly.

Use of Artificial Intelligence

Throughout the development of Gabbai, artifical intelligence was not used to generate code, text, or any assets. Throughout its maintenmance and expansion, Gabbai artificial intelligence will continue to play a minimal role into Gabbai's development and never be used to generate code. At times, a model was consulted when deciding how to best use APIs, advanced debugging, and testing.
