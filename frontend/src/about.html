Gabbai

Gabbai is an online system that allows a synagogue and its members to coordinate Shabbat and Yontif Torah readings in advance.

Gabbai keeps the Torah service moving   —  Gabbai builds a reading schedule unique to your congregation's traditions. A simple dashboard organizes who's reading what, opening registration to your members while retaining control and oversight for your shul.
